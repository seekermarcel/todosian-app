Todosian Fork, based on Todosian 2.2.

• Whole vault: optionally include notes in subfolders, shown by their path
• One-tap capture: the Add task widget can open a default file directly
• Custom statuses: tasks like [/], [-] or [w] are shown and can be set by long press
• Daily Focus includes tasks scheduled for today or earlier
• Saving a note keeps its line endings and final newline
